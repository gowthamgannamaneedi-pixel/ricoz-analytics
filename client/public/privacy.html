Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs data collection, handling, multi-tenant isolation, and protection standards for the RicozAnalytics web and Android platform (com.ricoz.analytics), operated by Ricoz Digital Innovations Pvt Ltd, Hyderabad, India.

🛡️ Zero Data Selling

We strictly never sell, monetize, or trade your corporate information or uploaded analytical datasets to third parties or advertising networks.

🔒 Multi-Tenant Isolation

Enterprise data is segregated by organization ID with cryptographic encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).

🗑️ Right to Deletion

Users can initiate complete account and dataset deletion at any time in-app or via our public request portal at ricoz.in/delete-account.

01. Personal Information We Collect

To administer enterprise analytics workspaces, we collect:

  • Account Profiles: Full name, corporate email address, organization/tenant name, and assigned permissions.
  • Authentication Credentials: Password hashes cryptographically salted via one-way bcrypt. Plaintext passwords are never stored or logged.
  • Customer Datasets: Tabular data files (CSV, Excel XLSX, JSON) voluntarily uploaded to your workspace for analytical indexing, metric computation, forecasting, and reporting.
  • Workspace Communications: In-app team messages, dataset comments, and AI Copilot query context within your organization.
  • Operational Telemetry: Audit logs (login timestamps, resource modifications, and export executions) for tenant security auditing.

Notice: RicozAnalytics does not collect device GPS location, phone contacts, photos/media libraries, biometric records, or third-party advertising identifiers.

02. Use of Information

We process your data strictly to execute contractual SaaS functionalities:

  • Authenticating users and isolating multi-tenant workspaces.
  • Aggregating datasets to render executive dashboards, charts, and forecasts.
  • Generating requested PDF and spreadsheet reports.
  • Delivering security notifications, OTP verification emails, and anomaly alerts.
  • Detecting service errors, enforcing latency performance, and mitigating security threats.

04. Android Mobile Permissions

The RicozAnalytics Android application (com.ricoz.analytics) declares only normal network permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required to establish encrypted HTTPS communication with RicozAnalytics cloud API servers.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to detect network connectivity changes and notify users when offline.

File exports (PDF, XLSX, CSV) use standard Android Scoped Storage (Downloads/Documents), requiring zero dangerous runtime permissions.

05. Retention & Account Deletion Policy

Data is maintained only as long as your workspace is active. Upon account deletion:

  • Purge: User profiles, credentials, uploaded datasets, and analytical caches are permanently purged within 30 days.
  • Statutory Retention: In accordance with statutory financial and corporate compliance requirements (e.g. Indian tax law), billing records and transaction audit trails are retained for up to 7 years in anonymized form.

06. Security Standards

All data in transit is protected via modern TLS 1.3 HTTPS encryption. All data at rest is protected by AES-256 block-level encryption. Multi-tenant access is enforced using Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and cryptographic JWT token authentication.
